### [Godot Frogger](https://github.com/localhost-chetan/godot-frogger)
A modern recreation of the classic Frogger game with enhanced mechanics.

**Features:**
- 🐸 Animated player sprite with directional movement (up, down, left, right)
- 🚗 Dynamic car spawning system with random colors and speeds
- 🎵 Background music & sound effects (car engine SFX)
- 📊 Score tracking & title screen with game flow
- 🏆 Goal zones and collision detection for win conditions
- 🗺️ Pixel art level with props, houses, trees, and decorative elements
- ⚙️ Collision layers system with proper physics handling

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.x, GDScript, AnimationPlayer

**Recent Work:**

- Score UI overlay with persistent CanvasLayer

- Level population with props and environmental assets

### [Godot Shooter](https://github.com/localhost-chetan/godot-shooter)
A top-down 2D shooter with dynamic lighting and advanced combat mechanics.

**Features:**
- 🎯 **Combat System**: Laser attacks, grenades, explosion mechanics
- 🎨 **Visual Polish**: Hit-flash shader, real-time color feedback on damage
- 👾 **Enemy Types**: Scout with chase AI, Drone with advanced combat behavior
- 💥 **Advanced Mechanics**:
  - AOE grenade explosions with damage radius
  - Invulnerability frames (mercy rule)
  - Health system capped at 100 HP
  - Breakable containers with item spawning via tweens
- 🎮 **Level Design**: Inside/outside areas with dynamic transitions
  - Breakable props: crates, toilets, beds, steam vents
  - Decals and environmental storytelling
- ⚡ **Signal-driven Architecture**: Globals setter system for stat management
- 🎬 **Animation**: Player animations tied to directional movement

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.x, GDScript, Shaders, Signal System, Tween Animation

**Recent Work:**
- Drone AI rewrite with full combat capabilities
- Hit-flash shader for visual feedback
- Health system with invulnerability mechanics
- Container break mechanics with item spawning
- Global signal refactor for cleaner stat management
